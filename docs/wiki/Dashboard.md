# Dashboard

The **Dashboard** is the executive command center of Ascend, delivering an institutional-grade overview of your multi-account, multi-currency portfolio alongside household owner snapshots in the navigation rail.

---

## 🎛️ Header, Global Filters & Sidebar Snapshot

At the top of the Dashboard, you are greeted with the current system date, data refresh status pill, and interactive **Report Filters**:

* **Greeting & Sync Status**: Displays a time-aware greeting (`Good Morning / Afternoon / Evening, <Name>`), system date, live **`Synced ...`** local update indicator alongside the exchange quote timestamp (**`Market data as of ...`**), automatic background sync badge, and a role-gated (`ADMIN` / `EDITOR`) **Refresh** trigger with cooldown protection.
* **Persistent Report Filters (`ReportFilters`)**: Dynamically scope the entire dashboard by:
  * **Investment Types** (e.g., Equity, ETF, Mutual Fund, Fixed Income, Crypto)
  * **Account Types** (e.g., TFSA, RRSP, Taxable, 401(k), IRA, Demat)
* **Session Persistence**: Filter configurations are persisted locally under `dashboard_filters` so customized views remain intact across browser sessions.

### Sidebar Owner Net Worth Snapshot (`SidebarOwnerSnapshot`)
Located directly in the persistent navigation sidebar (and collapsed icon rail tooltips on desktop):
* **Per-Owner Tiles**: Automatically groups active holdings by account owner (derived from each account's activity ledger owner) and displays each member's current Net Worth, period dollar/percentage change, and an SVG sparkline with a baseline reference line.
* **Configurable Timeframe Selector**: Compact inline dropdown supporting **`24H` (`1D`)**, **`1W`**, **`1M`**, **`YTD`**, **`1Y`**, and **`ALL`**, persisted in browser storage (`ascend_sidebar_snapshot_range`).
* **Interactive Sparkline Scrubbing**: Hovering across any owner's sparkline (or clicking into a tile) scrubs point-in-time valuation dates and dollar movements, powered by `/api/portfolio/owner-snapshot`.
* **Collapsible Group**: Collapse/expand the owner cards (`ascend_sidebar_owners_collapsed`) while keeping the combined portfolio header total visible.

## 💎 Tier 1: Executive Wealth Strip

The top KPI strip consolidates four decision-ready cards:

### 1. Total Net Worth (Hero Card)
* **Current Net Worth**: Consolidated market value of all active holdings across all platforms and accounts, converted into your active **Base Currency** (`USD`, `CAD`, `EUR`, `INR`, etc.).
* **Today's Change**: Absolute movement and percentage return since the previous market close:
  $$\Delta \text{Day} = \sum_{i=1}^{M} \left( \text{Quantity}_i \times (\text{Price}_{i, \text{today}} - \text{Price}_{i, \text{prev\_close}}) \times \text{FXRate}_{i, \text{today}} \right)$$
* **Footer Breakdown**: Displays **Invested Principal** (net investor cash excluding reinvested DRIPs), **Unrealized P&L ($ & %)**, and active **Scope** (`X Active • Y Accts`).

---

### 2. All-Time Profit
* **Total Profit ($ & TWR % Badge)**: Cumulative realized gains, unrealized capital appreciation, and lifetime dividends since inception.
* **Capital vs. Dividends Split Bar**: Visual progress bar showing the exact share of total profit generated by **Capital Appreciation** versus **Dividends**.

---

### 3. Annualized Return (`XIRR`)
XIRR is your **Dollar-Weighted Return (Money-Weighted Return)**. It solves for the annualized rate $r$ bringing the Net Present Value of all investor cash flows to zero via Newton-Raphson approximation:

$$\sum_{i=0}^{N} \frac{C_i}{(1+r)^{\frac{d_i - d_0}{365}}} = 0$$

* **Sub-metrics**: Shows **Unrealized ROI %** and cumulative **Realized Gains ($)** in the card footer.
* **DRIP Treatment**: First-class `DRIP` reinvestments do not double-count as fresh out-of-pocket contributions in cash-flow return calculations.
* **1-Year Age Guard (`<1Y`)**: If portfolio history is under 365 days, Ascend displays **`N/A (<1Y)`** instead of a distorted short-window annualized extrapolation.

#### Time-Weighted Return (TWR) & Daily Unitization Mechanics
TWR isolates underlying strategy performance from deposit/withdrawal timing:
1. **Day 0 (Inception)**: Base Net Asset Value (NAV) is initialized to $100.00$.
2. **Daily Unit Valuation**:
   $$NAV_t = NAV_{t-1} \times \left( \frac{\text{PassiveValue}_t}{\text{MarketValue}_{t-1}} \right)$$
3. **Cumulative Growth**:
   $$\text{TWR} = \left( \frac{NAV_{\text{current}} - NAV_{\text{inception}}}{NAV_{\text{inception}}} \right) \times 100\%$$

---

### 4. Passive Income & Yield
* **Projected Annual Income (`/ yr`)**: Forward-looking 12-month expected dividend income based on current share counts and declared dividend rates, alongside current **Portfolio Yield (%)**.
* **YTD Progress & Yield on Cost (YoC)**: Progress bar tracking **Received YTD** against projected annual payouts, plus **Yield on Cost (%)**.

## 📈 Tier 2: Unified Performance & Valuation Chart

The unified interactive chart card combines **Time-Weighted Return (%)** and **Portfolio Valuation ($)** trajectories:
* **Supported Ranges**: `1D` (intraday session curve), `1W`, `1M`, `3M`, `6M`, `YTD`, `1Y`, `2Y`, `3Y`, `5Y`, `ALL`, plus a custom start/end date picker.
* **Filter Synchronization**: Scoped automatically when Report Filters (`Investment Types` / `Account Types`) are active.

---

## ⚡ Tier 3: 3-Column Portfolio Pulse Row

### 1. Top Movers (1D Portfolio Impact)
Ascend ranks movers by **Portfolio Impact Percentage** rather than raw ticker percentage change, avoiding penny-stock distortion:

$$\text{Impact \%} = \frac{\Delta \text{Value}_{\text{asset, 1D}}}{\text{Total Portfolio Value}_{\text{prev\_day}}} \times 100\%$$

* **Top Gainers & Top Losers**: Highlights holdings contributing or detracting the most basis points today.
* **"View All" Modal (`TopMoversModal`)**: Full-screen breakdown of every constituent's 1D dollar move, impact %, position weight, and quick navigation to `/analysis/[symbol]`.

### 2. Allocation Snapshot
Interactive tabbed preview widget in the center pulse card:
* Switch instantly between **Sector**, **Geography**, **Type**, and **Accounts** breakdowns right from the Dashboard.
* Includes a direct **`Explore X-Ray →`** link to `/analysis/allocation`.

### 3. Dividend Summary
Provides immediate visibility into dividend distributions:
* **Received YTD** and **Projected Annual Income** converted to your base currency.
* **Upcoming Ex-Dividend Dates**: Chronological schedule of upcoming ex-dates (excluding sold dust positions and using activity/chart fallback metadata for ETFs when Yahoo quote summaries omit forward rates). Clicking any ticker navigates to its **[Asset Deep-Dive](Analysis-&-Reports#2-asset-deep-dive-analysissymbol)**.

## 🔄 Market Data Sync & Refresh Intervals

* **Automatic Background Sync (`src/instrumentation.ts`)**:
  * **Every 2 Minutes**: Incremental price & FX refresh (`runIncrementalSync({ respectMarketHours: true })`), using `2m` freshness during regular North American hours (`09:30–16:15 ET`, Mon–Fri) and `30m` off-hours/weekends.
  * **Every 6 Hours**: Metadata & historical OHLCV hydration (`runFullMarketSync({ force: false })`, skipping entries updated within the `12h` metadata / `24h` history TTLs).
* **Live UI Convergence & Manual Refresh**: Active tabs automatically revalidate on visibility/focus, reconnect, and cross-tab events (`useLiveSync`). `ADMIN` and `EDITOR` roles can trigger coalesced manual refreshes directly from the Dashboard or Asset Deep-Dive (`ADMIN` can inspect throttler diagnostics and run syncs under **Settings > Master Data**).
